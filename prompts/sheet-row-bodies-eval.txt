headScore = lowest cell for head (8-10 = every cell has a head; 1-2 = any head missing). feetScore = lowest cell for feet (same scale). fullBodyScore = lower of headScore and feetScore. In the reason, report per cell like "cellN: head=<yes|no>, feet=<yes|no>".

TASK 3b: COSTUME READS
Only when REQUESTED_COSTUME is given below. Ignoring the item list, judge whether a reader seeing this figure alone would name that costume. Every garment is worn the way that costume wears it. TASK 4: PROPORTIONS
Body proportions match the age stated as CHARACTER_AGE below — that stated age decides this score and outranks any impression of age read off the figure's face; when CHARACTER_AGE is unknown, judge against the figure's own apparent age instead. No oversized head on a small body, no adult shrunk to child proportions, and a figure drawn older or taller than the stated age scores ≤4. Score 1-10.

TASK 5: SINGLE SUBJECT — ONE FIGURE PER CELL
Each cell shows exactly ONE figure: the same single character in every cell. Any second figure anywhere in the row — another person, a partial body, a face in the margin, a figure of a different age or sex — is a defect. This includes faint, ghosted, semi-transparent or sketched-in figures, and figures overlapping or blended into the character. Cells must read as four separate figures with space between them, not a merged group. Any extra figure, or figures merged across cells = 1-3; one clean figure per cell = 9-10. soloScore 1-10.

TASK 6: PLAIN BACKGROUND
The area behind and around the figures must be plain white and empty (thin cell dividers are fine). Any painted scene, scenery, streets, walls, floor, sky, fog, or coloured/illustrated backdrop behind a figure = backgroundScore ≤ 2. Plain white/empty in every cell = 10. backgroundScore 1-10.
