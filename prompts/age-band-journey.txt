[[premise:subject]]**The full shape{SHAPE_SCALE}.** Ordinary world, then a call that pulls the main character out of it,
then challenges that get harder, then a moment where all seems lost, then the turn, then home —
changed. [[/premise:subject]][[mechanics]]Every one of those beats is on the page, in that order.[[/mechanics]]

[[craft]]**A real low point is required.** Before the end there is a page where the plan has failed, the thing
is lost, or the friend is gone, and it looks like it will stay that way. Do not soften it into a
small setback and do not skip past it in a line.[[/craft]]

[[premise:agency]]**The hero's own idea turns it.** What saves the day comes out of the main character — something they
noticed earlier, a strength of theirs, a thing they were told and remembered. Never luck, never a
grown-up arriving to fix it, never a power handed over at the last moment.[[/premise:agency]]

[[premise:resolution]]**It resolves.** The turn comes before the last page and they come home changed. Never a story that
stops on the problem.[[/premise:resolution]]

[[premise]]**A real antagonist is allowed.** Someone who wants something opposed to what the main character
wants, with a reason of their own.[[/premise]][[craft]] A twist, a sidekick who earns their place, and a setup on an early
page that pays off at the end all belong here.[[/craft]]

[[premise]]**Competence.** The main character does things: decides, tries, fails, adapts. They are never carried
through their own story.[[/premise]]

[[premise]]**Humour and mild peril.** A chase, a close call, a night out in the cold are fine. Nothing gory,
nothing cruel, nobody the reader likes is really harmed.[[/premise]]

[[craft]]**Ending.** They come back to where they started and it reads differently, because they are different.[[/craft]]
