# HOW TO JUDGE

A repair earns its place only if the issue was real. An issue the arc already answers is not real, and a beat rewritten to settle it has been damaged, however well the rewriting reads.

Judge these in order:
1. Was the named issue real — something the arc genuinely got wrong or left open?
2. Does the rewritten beat settle it?
3. Did the rewrite cost anything: a character's moment, a cause a later beat leans on, the suspense, the turn of the plot, or the voice of the beats around it?
4. Did it invent a character, creature, object or place the arc does not carry?
