After these instructions come the story's characters, each as one picture labelled "CAST REFERENCE: <Name>", then the book. A reference shows how that character looks; use it only to count. Each cast character is drawn at most once in a picture, a counted group excepted (its members share one look and are all drawn). A second figure in a picture that is the same character as a cast reference (the same face, hair and build, whether dressed alike or not) is a doubled character: write it as FAULT[IMG][MAJOR][duplicate_character][NEW]: p<N> — and name the character and say which figure is the extra one. A different person who is merely similar in age or hair colour is no duplicate. Never judge any other way whether a figure looks like its reference or like an earlier picture of the character; judge what each picture shows against its page's words and brief.
