

This book is reviewed once as a whole and every picture fault is also routed to the fix that suits it. Write the route as one more bracket after the type, so an IMG line reads "FAULT[IMG][<WEIGHT>][<TYPE>][<ROUTE>]: p<N> — <one sentence>". <ROUTE> is exactly one of:
- TOUCHUP — a local edit of the existing picture repairs it: an object added, removed or moved, a hand or gaze or expression changed, a colour or detail corrected, while the rest of the picture stays as it is. A local edit cannot change a garment, hair or the form of a face: those are FACE:<Name>, FIGURE:<Name> or NEW.
- NEW — the picture has to be drawn again: the wrong moment, the wrong place, several things wrong at once, a figure missing or doubled, or a fault no local edit can reach.
- FACE:<Name> — one named character's face or hair is wrong (wrong face, wrong hair, the character does not look like themselves) and redrawing only that character's head repairs it; the body, pose and outfit stay.
- FIGURE:<Name> — one named character's body, outfit, build or pose is wrong, or face and outfit both are, and redrawing that whole figure in the same place repairs it.
For FACE and FIGURE, <Name> is that character's name exactly as the brief lists it. Every fault on a page is repaired in the same round, one after another: the character repairs first, then the touch-ups; a page with a NEW line is drawn again instead, and the redraw is told every fault of that page.
Every NEW line also ends with the decision about the picture's background, written after the sentence as {BACKGROUND_TAG}. `background: keep` — the place, its light and its colours are right and fit the book; only what is drawn in front of them is wrong, so the picture is drawn again on the same background. `background: redo — <what to change>` — the background itself is wrong or does not belong to the book (the wrong place, a palette or lighting unlike the other pages, the wrong time of day, a missing or misplaced landmark); say in a few words what the new background must show or change. A NEW line with neither is not used. TOUCHUP, FACE and FIGURE lines carry no background decision.
The sentence after the dash is the instruction for that fix, written as an order to the painter ("Remove the second hat", "Draw the hero looking at the door"), with the cause left out. A cover is routed the same way. A TEXT line carries no route. A `height_order` fault is always routed NEW: no local edit re-sizes a figure.
