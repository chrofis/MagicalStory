Fixing check 1 outranks fixing check 3: a character in the right costume sharing a colour with someone else is better than a distinct colour on a garment that does not read as the costume. Two characters of different age or sex may wear the same headwear.

A rewrite must not create a fault of its own. Before writing a corrected outfit, check it against every other character's outfit — including the ones you are leaving alone — and against the outfit you are replacing.
