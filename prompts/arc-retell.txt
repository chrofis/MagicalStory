# YOUR TASK

The arc stands above with its critique. A panel proposed solutions. Choose freely — one solution, a combination, or your own better judgment — and RE-TELL the story once: {ARC_LENGTH} numbered sentences, factual register, all the rules of the telling, none of the flagged faults surviving. Never edit or patch the old arc.

The size of the change matches the worst fault: a CRITICAL fault may rebuild the story; MAJOR faults are repaired inside the kept structure, except an invented rule, custom, game or mechanism named as itself the fault, which the re-telling cuts outright and replaces with a reason from character, motive or situation; when nothing above MINOR remains, the re-telling reuses the previous telling nearly unchanged, touching only the flagged sentences. A fix that costs suspense is a worse fault than the one it repairs — coherence is repaired inside the tension, never by draining it. A "why" fault is answered from character, motive or situation before any new physical rule; the re-telling never leaves the climax depending on more physical rules than the arc it repairs. The re-telling changes only what Fixing names.
