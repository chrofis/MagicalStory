# Settled Verdicts

The one-page index of decisions that keep getting re-litigated. `docs/decisions.md` is the
archive (full Context/Decision/Rationale); this file is what a session actually reads before
editing prompts, eval rules, model routing, or pipeline behavior.

**Reversal protocol — reversing any line below requires ALL of:**
1. Explicit user sign-off (AskUserQuestion, framed as a reversal of a settled verdict).
2. Evidence: a Test Lab experiment ID, or ≥3 pages/stories showing the current rule failing.
   One motivating page is never sufficient.
3. A superseding entry in `docs/decisions.md` citing that evidence and linking the old entry.
4. Updating the line here.

Machine-checkable lines are enforced by `scripts/admin/check-settled.js` (runs in the pre-push hook).

## Prompts & evaluation

- **Text-overlay zone is CALM, never dark.** No "for white text" / dark-band language anywhere in prompts. *(guarded)*
- **Eval judges run at temperature 0, always.** Non-zero made tuning non-reproducible for months.
- **Pose mirrors (left/right body-part swaps) never deduct** in semantic eval.
- **Positions are relational** ("behind Roger", "on the boat"), not left/right-grid, except cross-page continuity / text-zone / ultra-wide needs.
- **Prompts are generic — no test-story names, characters, or settings.** Archetypes only. *(guarded)*
- **The "no hats" rules are gone** (2026-08-09) — do not reintroduce. *(guarded)*
- **Neon/saturation is never a deduction when the art style asks for it**; the style rule is style-conditional and passed per call.
- **Swiss orthography in every German string: ss never ß**; «guillemets» tight, no space before !?:; *(guarded)*
- **SOLID-GROUND rule: one canonical wording per prompt layer.**
- **Severity/deduction philosophy changes go to decisions.md** — flip-flops happen because the previous rationale wasn't findable.
- **Classification is the PROMPT's job; code may only change a severity.** Never pattern-match a finding's
  description text to decide what it MEANS (is this clothing? a mirror? an accessory?). Recognising "clothing"
  in prose does not generalise to complex stories — it works on the sample you tuned it against and fails on
  the next book. The sanctioned shape: the evaluator emits a **type** from the closed list, and code adjusts
  what that type may **cost** (`ZERO_POINT_TYPES`, `MAX_SEVERITY_TYPES` in `scoring.js`). A left/right regex
  guard was built and removed the same day, 2026-08-09; the prompt-side fix alone produced zero mirror findings.
- **The blind prompt-compliance judge is OFF — `promptComplianceJudge` defaults to `false`** (owner, 2026-09-19). It is Stage 2 of the eval misnamed "three-stage": the judge that never sees the picture and rules on it from Stage 1's text inventory (`prompts/image-prompt-compliance.txt`). When off the call **is not made**; `PROMPT_COMPLIANCE_JUDGE=true` re-arms it without a deploy. **STAGE 1 IS NOT GATED and still runs** — `runVisualInventory` is launched above the gate and the quality eval's own figure merge consumes it, feeding the empty-inventory score floor in `images.js`; gating Stage 1 would move scores, gating Stage 2 does not. **THE REASON IS PRECISION, NOT COST.** Two finding-by-finding audits against the pixels on `job_1789759147125_p08djwhbl`: pre-narrowing (8 pages, 46 findings, 528 points) **8.6% of its points defensible**, while it produced **64% of all deduction points and 16 of 24 false findings** against **1 false finding in 11** from the two judges that SEE the image; post-narrowing (Lab experiment **1333**, 8 pages) **3 keepers out of 29**, ~11% — **precision did not move**, attribution FALSE_JUDGE 12 / FALSE_STAGE1 6, so most errors survive even a perfect inventory. Its wrong findings buy paid repairs: an inpaint round spent recolouring trousers measured at `#202d40`, already the contract navy, and a false CRITICAL ordering a scarf back onto a neck the story empties on purpose. **So do not re-enable it to "recover coverage", and do not argue from the ~$0.35/story it also saves — the saving is a by-product and was never the case.** A reversal needs NEW PRECISION evidence (a re-run of 1333 or equivalent showing the keeper rate moved), not a new argument; full protocol. Absence is recorded per page as `notEvaluated {dimension: 'prompt_compliance', reason: 'compliance_judge_disabled'}` — an empty compliance bucket means NOT JUDGED, never JUDGED CLEAN. See decisions.md 2026-09-19.
- **The page TEXT is not a checklist for the image — semantic eval judges image-vs-BRIEF, by design** (owner, 2026-09-13). A page's prose may narrate two actions while the scene depicts one; the brief picks the moment. `image-semantic.txt` states this at lines 3/5/13/94 and it is correct — a page scoring 100/100 while the text mentions something absent is NOT a bug. Do not propose "compare the image against the page text" in the per-page evaluator, and do not re-open cast findings from text ("AD trims cast by design"). The reader's-eye text-vs-image comparison exists ONCE, at the end, in the book audit. See decisions.md 2026-09-13.
- **An object's OWN light is a state; the world's lighting of it is not** (owner, 2026-09-14). A thing that glows and goes dark, lights up and goes out, gets a `states[]` entry — a state is drawn as its own reference cell, and that cell is the only way a glowing look reaches the page; an emission held in `description` alone is on every page or none. The exclusion stays: a lamp falling on it, a shadow crossing it, dusk are the scene's, page by page, never a state. Stated three different ways across `scene-expansion-all.txt`, `story-unified.txt` and `story-trial.txt` before this; all three were brought into agreement, and `story-unified.txt` was deleted 2026-09-15 as unreachable, leaving the two live sites. *(guarded)* See decisions.md 2026-09-14.
- **Ask before coding an eval rule.** Prompt-vs-code is the owner's call, not an implementation detail —
  propose the shape first, then build.

## Pipeline & models

- **The 2026-05 scene composite is DEAD** (hardcoded kill-switch in server.js). Don't propose re-enabling THAT path — route-level composite for every page — without fixing style-drift, label leakage, aspect coercion AND an end-to-end gate.
- **The page composite IS enabled — for foreground+background scenes only** (owner, 2026-08-15). Grok gets depth wrong on those pages, and a scale-repair pass normally does not fix it: measured, scale repair fired on 42 pages in 30 days and left no artefact on any of them. The composite still has known issues — chiefly that the blend can relocate a character when the interaction text implies a different pose (Lab 727) — **but it is better than scale repair, which is the bar it has to clear.** Different entry point from the killed one (`storyJobPipeline` → `generateSceneComposite`, on the `needsScaleRepair` trigger); the kill-switch is untouched; two aborts keep the original render when detection or depth look wrong. Don't propose going back to scale repair without new evidence. Full rationale + Lab 653–727: decisions.md 2026-08-15.
- **The composite is INSURANCE, and firing rarely is the intended state** (owner, 2026-08-18). Measured over four stories after `scene-review` check 6c went in: **2 qualifying pages out of 52** — most stories now declare no `background` character at all, because 6c makes a writer earn the depth before declaring it. The owner's verdict: *"composite is mostly shit, so good that we do not run it often — but some pages might need it, so we still have it."* Two things follow: **don't widen the trigger** to make it run more, and **don't delete it as dead code** because it rarely fires. Both would be wrong for the same reason.
- **FLUX Dev/Schnell: rejected for page images.**
- **A Visual Bible cell's drawn AREA is NOT the object's size on the page — "make the reference cell bigger" is KILLED** (measured 2026-09-19). Evidence: `job_1789759147125_p08djwhbl` p7, 8 renders, $0.36, byte-identical stored production prompt (`diff` empty every run), md5-identical plate and 2-character slots, same character-card size, `grok-imagine-image-2.0`, through the production path (`buildPageCompositeRefs` → `packReferences` → `generateImageOnly`), ONE variable: the egg cell's drawn width. At the packer's **maximum** spread — 133 px vs 665 px, **25× area** — egg width ÷ nearest standing child height ran small 0.154/0.174/0.185 vs big 0.182/0.176/0.213: **overlapping ranges, one pair flat, 0-18% gained** where the target needs +90%. There is no headroom left to buy. Note the `scaleClass` enum (2026-09-15) is correct at the TEXT layer and also does not move the pixels — **scale is unexplained from both directions**, and the only untested layer is the PLATE (footprint painted into the empty scene). **Do not re-run the cell-size experiment.** Separately and still standing: never put a scale referent INSIDE a cell (owner, 2026-09-14) — cell contents leak onto the page. See decisions.md 2026-09-19.
- **A cramped VB cell DOES cost the object's identity — give every element enough drawn area to be READ** (same experiment, 2026-09-19, **3/3 vs 3/3**). Every small-cell arm rendered a generic grey-white egg; every big-cell arm rendered the reference's cream speckled egg. Framing/zoom varied within both arms, so it is not arm-linked. Cell area is an IDENTITY dial, never a SIZE dial — do not conflate the two verdicts above when quoting this line.
- **Style repair is OFF in production — `styleRepairProduction` defaults to `false` (owner, 2026-09-19, superseding the 2026-08-09 re-enable).** DETECTION IS UNCHANGED: `checkStoryStyleConsistency` sits above the flag in Step 5 and keeps writing the verdict and the outliers to `finalChecksReport.styleConsistency`, so a drifting book is still measured — only the paid repaint is gone. `STYLE_REPAIR_PRODUCTION=true` re-arms it without a deploy, and the Test Lab `style_repair` stage calls `repairPageStyle` directly and is deliberately not gated by the flag. The owner's reason is **cost and simplicity**: style drift is no longer considered a problem worth paying to repaint now that pages render on `grok-imagine-image-2.0`. **The measured evidence pointed the OTHER way and the owner decided anyway, with it in view** — run 4 (`job_1789759147125_p08djwhbl`): style repair ran on 3 covers only (never on any of the 18 pages), cost $0.06 of a $7.24 story, its own gate went 3 for 3 (`-1` better:after passedGate:true kept, `-2` better:after passedGate:true kept, `-3` better:same passedGate:false rejected), and all three covers were still logged as moderate style outliers ("Faces are rendered smoothly, lacking the specified brushstroke texture"). So **do not "restore" the default because the data looks favourable** — that is the argument that was already heard and overruled. Reversing this line needs the full protocol. See decisions.md 2026-09-19.
- **If it is re-armed, style repair runs on GROK** (`styleRepairModel`), settled 2026-08-24 after grok→gemini→grok. **Dormant in production while the flag above is false**; the setting still picks the model for the Test Lab `style_repair` stage, so the verdict below is live there and the moment the flag flips. **Neither model is reliable and neither ever will be by itself.** Gemini sometimes restyles fine and sometimes returns `IMAGE_OTHER` — a SAFETY refusal that yields no image, so the page silently keeps what it had. Its trigger is a content COMBINATION (owner's example: a young girl in a bikini as a mermaid), not the cast's age and not photorealism — do not write "Gemini refuses adults" anywhere. Grok no-ops on some pages, which is why 2026-08-09 flipped away from it. Grok is the default only because it won the pages actually measured (`IMAGE_OTHER` ×3 vs a correct repaint, two pages, plus Lab #837), and a no-op now loses to the comparative gate instead of shipping. **The real fix is a fallback across both models; it is not built.** Do not re-litigate the default — build the fallback.
- **Style repair is decided by its OWN comparative gate, never by finalScore** (owner, 2026-09-06). **STILL LIVE — turning the production repaint off does not retire this.** It is a rule about READING stored evidence, and every style-repair version already in the database, plus every Test Lab `style_repair` arm, is still judged this way. **It was tripped again on 2026-09-19.** `repairPageStyle` runs `compareStyleProximity(before, after)` and ships only on `better === 'after' && changed.length === 0`; the verdict is stored on the version as `styleRepair.passedGate` / `styleRepair.styleComparison`. Versions written before `0a3049bce` (2026-09-05) INHERITED the pre-repair `finalScore`, so a query over `imageVersions[].finalScore` shows every style-repair version tying its predecessor and looks like "0 improvements ever, can never ship". **That is not a defect and has now been raised as a false positive more than once — read `passedGate`, not the score.** A style repaint changes the medium, not the content; the ordinary per-image score cannot see what is being repaired.
- **Avatar passes 1 AND 2 default to Grok.** Gemini returns `IMAGE_OTHER` (a SAFETY refusal — no image) on a share of realistic-face inputs; the trigger is a content COMBINATION, **not the subject's age** — never write "Gemini refuses adults". The 2026-07-19 "Gemini stylises better" verdict was reversed 2026-08-06.
- **The unified writer call is NOT overloaded** — measured; don't split it without new measurements.
- **Beats is the pipeline, in every environment** — the value is a code constant in
  `server/config/runtime.js`, no longer a `PIPELINE_MODE` env var (2026-08-17). It decides which
  prompt file is live. Trials are always `unified` (speed); `inputData.pipelineMode` still overrides per job.
- **Grok inpaint handles structural changes** (pose, gaze, body rotation) — don't route facing-direction issues to iterate.
- **Page inpaint and iterate paint on the PAGE RENDER tier; char repair stays on Imagine 1.x** (owner, 2026-09-07, reversing 2026-09-06). The inpaint is a whole-frame edit with no mask — a Standard-tier edit of a 2.0 render erased a named animal's head and wiped real landmarks (decisions.md 2026-09-07). The Grok API has no region/mask parameter (measured). Pinning inpaint back to the edit tier is a reversal and needs the full protocol.
- **Repaired versions are evaluated against their OWN scene contract; finalScore is the one score everywhere.**
- **Cover gaze is code-owned: always at the viewer**; `gazes at:` is banned from cover hints.
- **Cover title text: model-baked EVERYWHERE — `coverTitleMode` = 'baked' (2026-09-06, supersedes the 2026-08-29 per-environment split).** The front cover renders the title INTO the artwork in ONE call on `coverTitleBakedModel` (`grok-imagine-2`); back and initial covers stay textless art. The flag reads through ONE resolver (`resolveCoverTitleMode`) so first generation, trials and every later repaint agree. Promotion to production was the owner's reversal call under the rule "production equals staging except repair rounds", with evidence: the 2026-08-29 5-story/5-style sample and the nine 2026-09-06 staging trial covers, all spelled correctly incl. umlauts and possessives (decisions.md 2026-09-06). `composited` (app-painted from a real font, spelling safe by construction) remains available as the fallback mode; returning production to it is a reversal and needs the full protocol.
- **Test Lab sets are generic, never per-stage** (a parallel title_sets mechanism was reverted).
- **Three Visual Bible elements per page — and LOCATIONS ARE NOT ELEMENTS** (owner, 2026-09-08, superseding the
  2026-09-06 count that included invented locations: "Do not count it as it is the empty scene not an artifact").
  `VB_ELEMENT_BUDGET` counts secondary characters, animals, artifacts and vehicles; a LOC, real or invented, never
  counts — the location is the plate the cast is composited into. The page's location cell rides LAST, at most one,
  3 + 1 = Grok's `VB_SLOT_MAX_ELEMENTS` of 4. The budget is enforced where the BRIEFS exist — the scene review's
  `rankPageElements` / `truncateBriefToBudget`, then the page-gen reference selection; do not re-add locations to
  `ELEMENT_COLLECTIONS` or to the AD's "all count" line. (The bible-time `trimVbAssignments` enforcer was removed
  2026-09-11: it decided from plan-line prose before any brief existed and emptied a story's central prop — see
  decisions.md "Visual Bible page assignment comes from the FINAL scene briefs". The COUNT and the
  locations-don't-count rule are unchanged and stay settled.)

## Data & infrastructure

- **No image bytes in JSONB — R2 only**, URL in `stories.data`. IRON RULE.
- **Clothing canonical source is the outline's `clothingRequirements`** (per story, per page) — never raw `avatars.clothing`.
- **DDL only via new `migrations/00N_*.sql`** — every other init path is dead.
- **Checkpoints are deliberately KEPT** — never propose deleting them.
- **Search ads land on the HOMEPAGE** — final, do not re-litigate.
- **Prod deploys: staging-first, per-push approval, pushes gated on an idle target environment.**
- **Timestamps shown to the owner: Swiss local (`… CH`) ONLY, via `scripts/lib/chTime.js` — UTC never shown, no hand arithmetic.** Settled 2026-08-09; supersedes UTC-only (May 2026), which superseded dual-labeling. Naive pg TIMESTAMP columns must be rehomed with `fromPgNaive()` before formatting.
- **Prop names are never rewritten in prompts; they are descriptive at the source.** `sanitizeVbIdsInPrompt` resolves Visual Bible IDS only. The name-for-type substitution (2026-08-24) and its exceptions were removed 2026-09-06 after four stories where it deleted the object or corrupted the cover title; a proper noun a story gives an object lives in `properName` and never reaches an image model. Reintroducing prose rewriting of prop names is a reversal.
- **One authored `label` per Visual Bible element is the only element string an image model sees; ids stay internal.** Settled 2026-09-13. `server/lib/vbLabel.js` is the single source; consumers read it through `elementLeadLabel` (image-facing) / `elementDisplayLabel` (judge-facing). Never add a new per-consumer naming rule; never emit a VB id to an image model (they get painted). Reversing needs the full protocol.
- **A character string becomes an entry only through `castResolver.resolveEntity`; two stored names compare only through `canonicalName`; ambiguity refuses, never guesses.** Settled 2026-09-13. Never add a per-consumer name matcher (equality, containment, subset, first-match) or a local VB-id regex — measured 32 double-listed rosters across 60 stories before this. Reversing needs the full protocol.
