# Settled Verdicts

The one-page index of decisions that keep getting re-litigated. `docs/decisions.md` is the
archive (full Context/Decision/Rationale); this file is what a session actually reads before
editing prompts, eval rules, model routing, or pipeline behavior.

**Reversal protocol — reversing any line below requires ALL of:**
1. Explicit user sign-off (AskUserQuestion, framed as a reversal of a settled verdict).
2. Evidence: a Test Lab experiment ID, or ≥3 pages/stories showing the current rule failing.
   One motivating page is never sufficient.
3. A superseding entry in `docs/decisions.md` citing that evidence and linking the old entry.
4. Updating the line here.

Machine-checkable lines are enforced by `scripts/admin/check-settled.js` (runs in the pre-push hook).

## Prompts & evaluation

- **Text-overlay zone is CALM, never dark.** No "for white text" / dark-band language anywhere in prompts. *(guarded)*
- **Eval judges run at temperature 0, always.** Non-zero made tuning non-reproducible for months. **Exception, Jev (2026-09-27, owner sign-off):** the Jev decision model has no temperature knob at all; its decisions are measured for flip rate, and the ones that flip (the group-page cast cuts, gaze) average 3 calls (`jevDecisions.JEV_DECISIONS`). See decisions.md 2026-09-27 "Jev decision layer wired".
- **Pose mirrors (left/right body-part swaps) never deduct** in semantic eval.
- **Positions are relational** ("behind Roger", "on the boat"), not left/right-grid, except cross-page continuity / text-zone / ultra-wide needs.
- **Prompts are generic — no test-story names, characters, or settings.** Archetypes only. *(guarded)*
- **The "no hats" rules are gone** (2026-08-09) — do not reintroduce. *(guarded)*
- **Neon/saturation is never a deduction when the art style asks for it**; the style rule is style-conditional and passed per call.
- **Swiss orthography in every German string: ss never ß**; «guillemets» tight, no space before !?:; *(guarded)*
- **SOLID-GROUND rule: one canonical wording per prompt layer.**
- **Severity/deduction philosophy changes go to decisions.md** — flip-flops happen because the previous rationale wasn't findable.
- **Classification is the PROMPT's job; code may only change a severity.** Never pattern-match a finding's
  description text to decide what it MEANS (is this clothing? a mirror? an accessory?). Recognising "clothing"
  in prose does not generalise to complex stories — it works on the sample you tuned it against and fails on
  the next book. The sanctioned shape: the evaluator emits a **type** from the closed list, and code adjusts
  what that type may **cost** (`ZERO_POINT_TYPES`, `MAX_SEVERITY_TYPES` in `scoring.js`). A left/right regex
  guard was built and removed the same day, 2026-08-09; the prompt-side fix alone produced zero mirror findings.
- **The blind prompt-compliance judge is OFF — `promptComplianceJudge` defaults to `false`** (owner, 2026-09-19). It is Stage 2 of the eval misnamed "three-stage": the judge that never sees the picture and rules on it from Stage 1's text inventory (`prompts/image-prompt-compliance.txt`). When off the call **is not made**; `PROMPT_COMPLIANCE_JUDGE=true` re-arms it without a deploy. **STAGE 1 IS NOT GATED and still runs** — `runVisualInventory` is launched above the gate and the quality eval's own figure merge consumes it, feeding the empty-inventory score floor in `images.js`; gating Stage 1 would move scores, gating Stage 2 does not. **THE REASON IS PRECISION, NOT COST.** Two finding-by-finding audits against the pixels on `job_1789759147125_p08djwhbl`: pre-narrowing (8 pages, 46 findings, 528 points) **8.6% of its points defensible**, while it produced **64% of all deduction points and 16 of 24 false findings** against **1 false finding in 11** from the two judges that SEE the image; post-narrowing (Lab experiment **1333**, 8 pages) **3 keepers out of 29**, ~11% — **precision did not move**, attribution FALSE_JUDGE 12 / FALSE_STAGE1 6, so most errors survive even a perfect inventory. Its wrong findings buy paid repairs: an inpaint round spent recolouring trousers measured at `#202d40`, already the contract navy, and a false CRITICAL ordering a scarf back onto a neck the story empties on purpose. **So do not re-enable it to "recover coverage", and do not argue from the ~$0.35/story it also saves — the saving is a by-product and was never the case.** A reversal needs NEW PRECISION evidence (a re-run of 1333 or equivalent showing the keeper rate moved), not a new argument; full protocol. Absence is recorded per page as `notEvaluated {dimension: 'prompt_compliance', reason: 'compliance_judge_disabled'}` — an empty compliance bucket means NOT JUDGED, never JUDGED CLEAN. See decisions.md 2026-09-19.
- **The page TEXT is not a checklist for the image — semantic eval judges image-vs-BRIEF, by design** (owner, 2026-09-13). A page's prose may narrate two actions while the scene depicts one; the brief picks the moment. `image-semantic.txt` states this at lines 3/5/13/94 and it is correct — a page scoring 100/100 while the text mentions something absent is NOT a bug. Do not propose "compare the image against the page text" in the per-page evaluator, and do not re-open cast findings from text ("AD trims cast by design"). The reader's-eye text-vs-image comparison exists ONCE, at the end, in the book audit. See decisions.md 2026-09-13.
- **An object's OWN light is a state; the world's lighting of it is not** (owner, 2026-09-14). A thing that glows and goes dark, lights up and goes out, gets a `states[]` entry — a state is drawn as its own reference cell, and that cell is the only way a glowing look reaches the page; an emission held in `description` alone is on every page or none. The exclusion stays: a lamp falling on it, a shadow crossing it, dusk are the scene's, page by page, never a state. Stated three different ways across `scene-expansion-all.txt`, `story-unified.txt` and `story-trial.txt` before this; all three were brought into agreement, and `story-unified.txt` was deleted 2026-09-15 as unreachable, leaving the two live sites. *(guarded)* See decisions.md 2026-09-14.
- **Character presence is three cases from the evaluator's match data** (owner, 2026-09-24, reversing 2026-09-13): a cast name no figure claims with no unmatched figure → ADD (`missing_character`); every name claimed with an unmatched figure → REMOVE (`extra_character`; **since 2026-10-07 a page redo (iterate), not an inpaint** — owner, 39-story evidence iterate 22/23 vs inpaint 12/18, decisions.md 2026-10-07 "Re-staging defects"; `missing_character` and `setting` route the same way — a declared-empty cast included); both → the unmatched figure IS the missing member drawn wrong → REDRAW it as them (`character_identity` + figure id → figure-targeted char-fix, never a deletion). Unequal counts pair deterministically (figure-id order vs cast order, photo-less names first). Decided in `derivePresenceFinding` from structured data, never from finding text. See decisions.md 2026-09-24.
- **Ask before coding an eval rule.** Prompt-vs-code is the owner's call, not an implementation detail —
  propose the shape first, then build.
