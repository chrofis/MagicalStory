# Labelling rubric (written before reading any card; single labeller = the implementing agent, blind to arm and Jev scores)

Binary per card. Cell context (age, topic, theme) is shown. NOT defects (owner 2026-10-09): idea does not resolve, characters appear without introduction, worlds mix.

- enjoy   1 if a child of that age would plausibly want to hear it: the main character wants something a child that age cares about and does something playful, active or imaginative; picturable at once. 0 if the want is a chore/abstract, the plot cannot be pictured, or it is a lecture.
- buy     1 if a parent of that age would pay for it as a book about their child: the commissioned topic (if any) and the theme are both recognisable, it is gentle and age-suitable, nothing odd or confusing. 0 if the topic/theme is missing, preachy, or the card reads confusing.
- charcon 1 if the main character stays one coherent character: one name, one role (costume role not swapped), acts that suit the stated age and gender, no sudden change of who they are or what they want. 0 otherwise.
- agency  1 if an outside obstacle stops the main character and the character themself does something that deals with it. 0 if others/luck solve it, nothing stops them, or the act does not touch the obstacle.
- agefit  1 if the want, the act and the vocabulary suit the stated age (a 3-year-old gets reach/hold/follow, a 12-year-old gets real stakes). 0 if clearly too young or too old.
- topic   1 if the commissioned topic (life-challenge cards only; adventure = null) shows up as the thing the character works on in sentence 2 (an act that is the topic, not a bystander).
- theme   1 if the stated theme is visible in the card as setting/role/props (null if no theme).

Existing labels (hand, 2026-10-09 coherence run) are kept as forced / follows.
