1. [MAJOR] (s2-s23) STAKES: Nothing in the story ever resists Ludovica: the logic gives no loss and no deadline, the opposition is only "the width of the sea", and twenty-odd stops repeat one call and one animal gesture with no rising cost, wrong turn or choice until the last shell, so the story raises no question that tightens — "nothing is lost if she does not, she has simply not said ciao yet; there is no deadline" … "At a red sponge with many holes, Ludovica calls «Nerina, sei lì dentro?» and a blue-striped fish peeks out of each hole". Smallest change: cut most of the repeated stops (s11-s23) and let one stop go wrong in a way that costs Ludovica something, pointing to the Facts line that Nerina hears only from a few steps away.
2. [MAJOR] (s3, s8, s25) CAST: Mamma's only acts are holding the towel, warning that the stones are slippery and wrapping Ludovica afterwards, and none of them changes what happens next (everything coming out of the grotto is dry at once anyway) — "Mamma holds the towel under Ludovica's tail while she leans over" … "Mamma tells Papà that the wet stones are slippery" … "where everything is dry at once and Mamma wraps her in the towel". Smallest change: give Mamma one act on her Motives line that opens a way in the search, or cut the towel she does not need.
3. [MINOR] (s17) ORPHAN: The clam that shows a white pearl and shuts is a plant that nothing later uses — "it opens a little to show a white pearl and claps shut".
