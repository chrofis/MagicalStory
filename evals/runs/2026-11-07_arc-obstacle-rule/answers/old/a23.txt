[MAJOR] (s1, s10) CAST: Aldric, the commissioned wizard, only warns and watches: he forbids the door, loads the cart, "counts the year finished" and plans to rehang the door in the morning, and nothing he does changes what happens next — "an apprentice finds his own words, and that the door stays shut" … "Aldric has watched the glow through the bars and counts the year finished" Smallest change: give Aldric one act in the middle or at the gate that changes the outcome, or cut the claim that he is the one who decides the year.
2. [MAJOR] (s1, s6, s10) STAKES: the question the story raises, why the apprentice must "find his own words" and the door stays shut, is never answered; Lukas reads the seventh line from the forbidden book and Aldric then counts the year finished with no word on the book, the rule or the broken ban — "an apprentice finds his own words" … "counts the year finished". Smallest change: point s10 at the s1 reason, so that Aldric's verdict names it.
3. [MAJOR] (s4, s8, s10) CAST: Sophie is given a want and a quarrel, anger at the keys and at the shut gate, and the ending never answers either in an act or line of hers; she keeps no later use for her loss and her fury — "she is so angry that she kicks the door" … "Sophie shouts that this is unfair". Smallest change: give Sophie an act or line at the gate or the long table that answers her anger or her lost keys.
