[MAJOR] (s3, s4) STAKES: The opposition is robbed of its power at the turning point by two limits the logic hands it, so the captain who "stomps over to stop the children" is beaten by a Facts line, not by anything the children do — "cannot catch a parrot that is flying free" … "Rico shoots out past the captain's grabbing hand". Smallest change: cut the Facts line "cannot catch a parrot that is flying free" and the line that the ship "cannot turn back in the narrow Limmat".

[MAJOR] (s1, s4) CAST: Daniel and Sarah are commissioned adults whose only part is waving and permitting the trip, and no act of theirs changes what happens next — "Mama Sarah and Papa Daniel wave from the Limmatquai" … "Back on the Limmatquai with Daniel and Sarah". Smallest change: give Daniel or Sarah one act on the Motives line that changes the rescue, instead of "waits and waves".
