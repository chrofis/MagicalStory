1. [MAJOR] (s11, s14) CLAIM: The climax cannot work as stated, since Fiona cuts her coat into strips and knots them, which cuts across the doubled seams she trusts, and then scrapes a stone loose and digs a chest out of an old wall with sewing scissors — "cuts her own coat into strips with her sewing scissors and knots them into a rope, because she knows her doubled seams will hold her" … "scrapes the old mortar from the stone with her scissors, digs the small chest out of the earth behind it". Smallest change: name in the Facts how the strips keep her seams and how the stone comes out.
2. [MAJOR] (s15) STAKES: The question of who keeps the treasure is left open, since the chest is opened on the landing in front of Herr Brunner and the chart goes back to the museum, but nothing says who keeps the chest or the coins — "the friends open the chest on a heap of old silver coins; Fiona hands Herr Brunner the chart, which belongs to his museum". Smallest change: name in s15 whose the chest is.
3. [MAJOR] (s15) ENDING: The pirate crew and Pfeffer carried the friends the whole way, yet the last sentence gives them no thanks or goodbye and ends on Brunner and a coat — "the Seeschwalbe fades into the fog" … "Fiona stands in half a coat and laughs". Smallest change: give the crew or Pfeffer a closing line in s15 before the ship fades.
