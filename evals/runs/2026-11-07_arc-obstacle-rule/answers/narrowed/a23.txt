1. [MAJOR] (s1, s5, s6) DEVICE: Aldric, who knows exactly where the seventh line is and could simply say it or open the door, withholds both behind a rule ("an apprentice finds his own words") that only exists to keep the book shut, and a child asks at once why the wizard does not just tell him or unlock his own door — "an apprentice finds his own words, and that the door stays shut" … "the key has been missing since the summer storm". Smallest change: cut the "finds his own words" rule from s1 and let Aldric only say he is busy and the key is lost.
2. [MAJOR] (s4, s8, s10) CAST: Sophie's quarrel is given and never answered — she kicks the door in anger and shouts at the gate, but the ending (Frau Halder, Manuel, Lukas, Eli at the table) gives her no act or line, so her anger and her complaint about unfairness are simply dropped — "she is so angry that she kicks the door" … "when Sophie shouts that this is unfair she folds her arms and turns her back". Smallest change: give Sophie a line or act in s10 that answers her shout about unfairness.
3. [MAJOR] (s6, s7) REPLACEABLE: the children set out at dusk for a lantern festival from a wizard's tower with no lamp, candle or lantern of their own, and nothing says why the only light is the phone left on the sill or why they do not turn back for it or take any other light — "the Natel stays lying on the windowsill, therefore they have no light on the steep wet steps". Smallest change: state in s6 why no other light is to hand, or have them fetch the Natel and cut the loss.
