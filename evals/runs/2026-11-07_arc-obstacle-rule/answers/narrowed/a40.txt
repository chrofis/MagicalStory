[MAJOR] (s12, s13, s16) STAKES: the opposition, Brummbart's sailing on, is undone by Yara's "kree" and the gull's answer, not by any act of Eliah, whose own call comes only after the anchor is already down and answers a different problem, the gull's blindness — "Brummbart stops the hauling, drops the anchor" … "Eliah takes his hands off his cap". Smallest change: use the Motives line for Brummbart, which already has him drop the anchor "when the gull answers", and let Eliah's act, not Yara's, bring that answer.
2. [MINOR] (s3, s16) ASSUMED: Eliah's "rhyme" that the gull answers and that he calls at the climax is never given, so the reader cannot tell what recognises him — "when he says his rhyme she tilts her head" … "calls her name and his rhyme in a clear voice".
3. [MINOR] (s8, s17) SENSE: a gull that cannot fly is said to hop after a gliding ship through reeds and fog and to be "right beside the ship" the moment it is needed — "with Spaghetti hopping after it unseen" … "from the reeds right beside the ship".
