---PAGE PLAN---
Page 1: SHOT — Hans, Berta — Hans kneels beside Berta at the water's edge and tucks his letter for Mira under the edge of her shell — Berta carries the letter and waits for the tide.
Page 2: SHOT — Berta, Emma — Berta tilts her shell toward Emma and Emma's hand closes on Opa Hans's letter — Emma holds the letter and will carry it herself.
Page 3: SHOT — Sarah, Daniel, Emma, Noah — all four pull the blanket out flat over the sand, Emma's blue sea-glass in her fist — the blanket lies spread on the beach and the family is settled by the reef.
Page 4: SHOT — Emma, Noah, Berta — Emma, her sea-glass in her other hand, and Noah take hold of Berta's flipper in the surf — both children have tails and breathe under water.
Page 5: SHOT — Noah, Emma — Noah shouts from the wreck's narrow gap, his broad tail fin pressed against the hull, Emma beside him — Noah is stuck outside, the gap is the short way to Mira.
Page 6: SHOT — Oskar, Emma — Oskar pulls the letter deep into a dark crack of the hull with his arms while Emma reaches after it — the letter is hidden deeper and Oskar wants a playmate.
Page 7: SHOT — Emma — Emma floats alone in the dark hull, her hands cupped at her mouth, calling for Mira — her shout has not carried and the letter is still lost.
Page 8: SHOT — Daniel, Berta — Daniel cups his hands and calls out over the water toward the reef while Berta lifts her head to the sea — the orange evening light has begun.
Page 9: SHOT — nobody — the first orange beam falls through a hole in the planks and shimmers on the wall of the dark hull — a moving light now reaches the inside of the hull.
Page 10: SHOT — Emma, Oskar — Emma holds her sea-glass in the orange beam so the dot dances across the wall, Oskar's arms chasing it — Oskar has let go of the letter.
Page 11: SHOT — Emma, Oskar — Emma wedges her sea-glass into the hole in the planks, the letter in her other hand, Oskar's arms following the dot — the sea-glass stays with Oskar and Emma holds the letter.
Page 12: SHOT — Mira — Mira sings one note in the surf in the last orange light — her song has reached the dune bench and Hans's question is answered.
Page 13: SHOT — Sarah, Daniel, Emma, Noah — Sarah and Daniel wrap the blanket around Emma and Noah on the sand — both children are warm and back with their parents.
Page 14: SHOT — Emma, Mira, Hans, Berta — Emma, Mira and Hans, seen from behind, wave to Berta, who raises one flipper in the sea — the tide has lifted Berta and she swims away.
