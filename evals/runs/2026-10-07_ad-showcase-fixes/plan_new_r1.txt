---PAGE PLAN---
Page 1: SHOT — Emma, Berta, Hans — Emma lifts Opa Hans's letter off Berta's shell at the water's edge while Hans on the dune bench watches — Emma holds the letter and means to carry it herself; Berta stays on the sand.
Page 2: SHOT — Daniel, Sarah, Emma — Daniel, Sarah and Emma spread the blanket on the sand — the family has made camp on the beach, within sight of the reef.
Page 3: SHOT — Berta, Emma, Noah — Emma, her blue sea-glass in her other hand, and Noah hold Berta's flipper in the surf — Emma and Noah have tails and breathe under water.
Page 4: SHOT — Noah, Emma — Noah pushes his broad tail fin at the narrow gap in the wreck's hull while Emma holds his hand — Noah's fin does not fit the gap, so only Emma can go in.
Page 5: SHOT — Emma, Oskar — Oskar's arm snatches the letter from Emma's hand inside the dark hull — Oskar holds the letter and Emma is alone in the hull.
Page 6: SHOT — Emma — Emma cups her hands to her mouth and calls for Mira into the dark hull — the shout stops at the planks, the letter is still lost, and Papa's call has come from the shore.
Page 7: SHOT — Berta, Hans — Berta digs both flippers into the sand toward the sea in the first orange evening light while Hans watches from the bench — the tide has not yet reached Berta, and the orange light has begun.
Page 8: SHOT — nobody — A beam of orange sun falls through a hole in the hull planks and shimmers on the inner wall — the hull has light that moves.
Page 9: SHOT — Emma, Oskar — Emma holds her sea-glass in the orange beam so the dot of light dances across the wall and Oskar's arms chase it — Oskar has let go of the letter.
Page 10: SHOT — Emma — Emma wedges her sea-glass in the hole in the planks, the letter in her other hand — the sea-glass stays behind with Oskar and Emma has the letter.
Page 11: SHOT — Mira, Emma — Mira reads the letter among the red coral in her cove while Emma is beside her — Mira has Hans's letter and laughs.
Page 12: SHOT — Hans, Mira — Hans stands up from the dune bench and waves toward Mira, who sings one note in the surf — Hans has his answer.
Page 13: SHOT — Sarah, Daniel, Emma, Noah — Sarah and Daniel wrap Emma and Noah in the blanket at the water's edge in the last orange light — the children are back with their parents, warm.
Page 14: SHOT — Berta, Emma, Noah, Sarah, Daniel — Berta raises one flipper as the rising tide lifts her while Emma, Noah, Sarah and Daniel, seen from behind, wave after her — Berta has swum away to the sea.
