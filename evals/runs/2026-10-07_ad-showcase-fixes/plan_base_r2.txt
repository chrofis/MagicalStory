---PAGE PLAN---
Page 1: SHOT — Hans, Berta — Hans shades his eyes toward the sea from the bench while Berta stretches her neck toward the water, the letter on her shell — Opa Hans waits for Mira's answer; Berta lies stranded at the water's edge with his letter.
Page 2: SHOT — Emma, Berta — Berta tilts her shell toward Emma, who closes both hands round Opa Hans's letter on it — Emma holds the letter and will carry it herself.
Page 3: SHOT — Sarah, Daniel — Sarah and Daniel shake out the blanket and spread it on the sand — the family's place on the beach is set.
Page 4: SHOT — Berta, Emma, Noah — Berta holds out her flipper in the surf; Emma and Noah grip it, the sea-glass closed in Emma's other hand, a dot of light on the wet sand — Emma and Noah have tails and breathe under water, and Emma holds her sea-glass.
Page 5: SHOT — Noah, Emma — Noah swims in front straight across the reef toward the wreck, Emma swimming after him — they are on the short way, away from Berta's arc.
Page 6: SHOT — Noah, Emma — Noah presses his broad tail fin into the narrow gap in the hull while Emma holds his hand — his fin does not fit; only Emma's small fin can pass.
Page 7: SHOT — Emma, Oskar — Oskar's arm coils round the letter in Emma's hand in the dark hull — Oskar has taken the letter and is hiding it.
Page 8: SHOT — Emma — Emma calls with her mouth wide open in the dark hull, her hands bare of the letter — her shout does not carry past the wreck; the letter is lost and she is alone.
Page 9: SHOT — Daniel, Berta — Daniel cups his hands round his mouth and calls over the water in the first orange light, Berta stretching her neck toward the sea beside him — the call has come and the daylight is nearly out.
Page 10: SHOT — nobody — The first orange beam falls through a hole in the planks and shimmers on the dark hull wall — the first light inside the hull, something that can be made to move.
Page 11: SHOT — Emma, Oskar — Emma tilts her sea-glass in the orange beam; the dot of light dancing on the wall draws Oskar's arms after it — Oskar has let go of the letter and Emma takes it.
Page 12: SHOT — Mira, Emma — Mira unfolds and reads the letter in the red coral cove, laughing, Emma beside her — Mira has Opa Hans's letter and will go to the surf.
Page 13: SHOT — Mira, Hans, Emma, Noah, Sarah, Daniel — Seen from behind, Hans, Emma, Noah, Sarah and Daniel watch Mira sing a note in the surf in the last orange light — Mira has sung, Hans is on his feet, the family is wrapped in the blanket.
Page 14: SHOT — Emma, Noah, Sarah, Daniel, Hans, Berta — Seen from behind, the family and Hans wave at Berta, who raises one flipper as the tide lifts her and she swims away — Berta is afloat and gone with the tide, and Emma has promised to see her next autumn.
