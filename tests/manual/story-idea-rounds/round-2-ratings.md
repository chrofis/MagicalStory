## The next three worst classes and a proposed fix for each

Not applied.

### 1. Middle or solution leaked into the premise (11/20, unmoved)

Quoting was not enough: the model quotes the decider and then ships it. The check has to bind
[FINAL], not [REVIEW]. Replace check 6 in both templates:

> 6. **Back cover, not synopsis**: quote the noun a reader would expect to decide the outcome and
>    the sentence that says what happens next. Neither quoted string may appear in [FINAL]. If
>    nothing can be quoted, write "nothing to cut".

and add one line to RULES and to REQUIRED:

> - The last sentence states what failing costs. Nothing comes after it.

### 2. Peril over the line (4/20, up from 3; #7 sits at g=1 in both rounds)

The peril rule is still a yes/no question and is not tied to the youngest character. Replace
check 7 in both templates:

> 7. **Peril**: name the youngest character and their age, then quote the words that put them at a
>    height, in deep or rising water, in the dark underground, or in a vehicle with a supply running
>    out. Replace that place in [FINAL].

### 3. Output-format drift — the English `ROLES:` label (2/20, both historical participant ideas)

`prompts/story-idea-requirements-historical-1.txt` hard-codes `ROLES:` in its format block, so a
German idea opens with an English header. Make the label follow the output language:

> Open with the role list under a one-word heading in the output language, then the idea.
