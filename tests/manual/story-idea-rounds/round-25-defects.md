## Cast join — 2

- **Cell 1 arm 1** — "Noah und **sein Vater** steigen auf das Schiff". The cast is
  Noah alone. A father is not a keeper standing in the way; he is a companion, and
  the rule is that a new companion does not join.
- **Cell 7 arm 2** — "zieht **eine Nachbarin** Lena Stück für Stück an". The cast is
  Lena alone. A grown-up has to be present at that age (the topic guide requires
  one), but the text gives that grown-up an identity the cast does not contain.
  Cell 7 arm 1 does the same job correctly with "jemand hält ihre Hand".

Keepers and strangers who stand in the way are NOT counted here — they are allowed:
"le gardien du château" (cell 9 arm 1), "Der Mann, der die Maschine bewacht" (cell 8
arm 2), "Herr Keller ... steht vor dem Anzugschrank" (cell 5 arm 2, and he is cast).

## Peril — 3 mild, 0 severe

No child is in bodily danger anywhere in the round. Three carry a menace the band's
age makes worth naming:

- **Cell 4 arm 1** (main aged 9) — red alarm, sixty seconds of fuel, a child

## Invented words — 1, plus a typography fault on every toddler arm

- **Cell 7 arm 1 — "Scheunenkatz"**. Not a German word; the noun is
  *Scheunenkatze*. Same class as round 23's «schnatschert».
- Everything else parses as real German or French. The compounds that look coined
  are legal: *Mondposten-Funk*, *Anzugschrank*, *Anzugärmel*, *Drachenschuppe*,
  *Zaubermantel*, *Landebefehl*.

**Typography, all four 0-2 arms:** every refrain is introduced with a colon and set
bare, not in Swiss guillemets — "und fragt: Hörst du es?" (7/1), "Noch eines, und
raus." (7/2), "ruft ihr nach: Wart auf mich!" (11/1), "ruft jedes Mal mit ihm: Wo
bist du?" (11/2). Round 24 produced the same shape with straight double quotes.
The refrain is the one line of a toddler book a parent says out loud; it should be
«…». Not present on any 3+ arm, because no 3+ arm quotes speech at all.
