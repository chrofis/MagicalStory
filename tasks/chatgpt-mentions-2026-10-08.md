# Getting MagicalStory mentioned where ChatGPT looks (2026-10-08)

Research only. No code was changed. Every site, contact and price below comes from a page that was opened on 2026-10-08, and the source URL is cited. Anything that could not be opened is marked **unverified**. No rate was estimated: where a site publishes no price, the table says "no published rate".

## Key findings

1. **Most "best of" lists that rank for our head terms are written by competitors about themselves.**
   - buchkraft.ch: "Die 5 besten Plattformen…" is written by the "Buchkraft Team" and puts Buchkraft in its own list ([source](https://buchkraft.ch/blog/beste-plattformen-personalisierte-kinderbuecher)). Buchkraft is a **Swiss AI book competitor** (fluxie's summary lists 39 CHF hardcover, 19 CHF e-book). It is not a neutral roundup.
   - The same is true of fluxie.de ([source](https://fluxie.de/home/blog/personalisiertes-kinderbuch/)), lullaby.ink ([source](https://lullaby.ink/de/blog/bester-ki-kinderbuch-generator-2026), written by Lullaby's founder) and, in French, contedia.fr, lepetitheros.com and pourquipourtoi.fr.
   - These pages will never add us. Two things work instead: getting into the few **independent** lists, and keeping our own honest comparison pages current. Those pages already exist: `client/src/constants/comparisonData.ts` covers Librio, Wonderbly, BuchHeldenWelt, Magisches Kinderbuch, Lullaby, LoveToRead, Framily and Hooray Heroes. Buchkraft (a Swiss AI book service), myposter KI-Kinderbuch and the FR players (Contedia, Le Petit Héros) are not covered yet.
2. **The Trustpilot audit is out of date.** https://www.trustpilot.com/review/magicalstory.ch exists, was **claimed in March 2026**, and has **1 review** (TrustScore 3.7). For comparison, Librio has 847 reviews at 4.8 ([source](https://de.trustpilot.com/review/librio.com)) and magicstory.com has about 205 at 4.5. The free plan allows 50 invitations per month ([source](https://business.trustpilot.com/plans)).
3. **mothersfinest.org is not a parenting blog.** It is a Munich postpartum meal delivery company (Mothers Finest GmbH, kontakt@mothersfinest.org). The audit's claim that it reviews Librio could not be confirmed, so it is dropped as a target.
4. **Hostile climate in Swiss literary media.** SRF covered the protest by SBVV, A*dS and syndicom against Swisscom's AI children's book ([SRF](https://www.srf.ch/kultur/gesellschaft-religion/debatte-um-ki-kinderbuch-aufschrei-gegen-neue-swisscom-kampagne), [derStandard](https://www.derstandard.at/story/3000000281536/protest-gegen-ki-generiertes-kinderbuch-der-swisscom)). Pitch parenting media on the **child's problem** (sibling fights, new baby, moving) and not on "AI book". Avoid book trade and literary press.
5. **Google Business Profile probably does not fit.** Eligibility requires a business that "has a physical location that customers can visit, or travels to customers where they are" ([source](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177?hl=en)). An online-only service does not meet that. Do not create one with a virtual address, because the guidelines forbid it.
6. **Most AI-tool directories now charge for submission, and some sell links.**
   - topai.tools: USD 47 one-time ([source](https://topai.tools/submit)).
   - toolmage.com: USD 69, "no free option" ([source](https://www.toolmage.com/en/submit-you-ai/)).
   - huntscreens.com: its submit page sells "dofollow backlink" add-ons ([source](https://huntscreens.com/submit)). **Skip it:** that is link buying.
   - Product Hunt is free ("It's 100% free to use", [source](https://www.producthunt.com/launch)).
   - **Owner decision needed:** is a paid, editorially reviewed directory listing "buying audience" (OK) or "buying links" (not OK)?
7. **Not verifiable here:**
   - Reddit threads: the search tool returned none.
   - ChatGPT's actual citations: these cannot be queried from here. The owner should run the five target queries in ChatGPT with search turned on and record which domains it cites. That is the real ground truth for this plan.
   - theresanaiforthat.com, toolify.ai and futurepedia.io: the pages blocked us (403/404), so their submit terms are **unverified**.
   - schweizerfamilienblogs.ch (the Swiss family blogger network, 58 blogs per [kleinreport](https://www.kleinreport.ch/news/schweizer-familienblog-mit-neuer-plattform-und-ehrenkodex-96660/)): the domain did **not resolve** today. Its status is unverified.

## Steps

1. **Week 1: baseline and reviews (free)**
   - Run the five target queries in ChatGPT (search on), Perplexity and Google AI Overviews. Save the cited domains in this file. That is the baseline to re-check monthly.
   - Turn on Trustpilot invitations for every delivered book (50 per month free) and reply to every review.
   - Do not create a Google Business Profile (finding 5).
2. **Week 1–2: owned content**
   - Extend the comparison pages with Buchkraft (Swiss, AI, closest direct competitor), myposter KI-Kinderbuch, and a FR comparison (Contedia, Le Petit Héros, Wonderbly).
   - Keep the comparisons honest and dated. LLMs quote these pages, and competitors already do exactly this.
3. **Week 2: free editorial pitches** (template DE/FR below, plus a free review book)
   - DE: moms.de, familie.de, connect.de, papaseite.de, Mamalicious "testet".
   - FR: coreanne, babynounou.
   - Lead with one life-challenge story, for example sibling fights or a new baby.
4. **Week 2: Product Hunt launch** (free), and a **Startupticker** pitch if the company meets their criteria (Swiss, incorporated, one full-time founder) and has a real news hook.
5. **Week 3–4: paid audience, clearly labelled**
   - Request media kits from swissmom, Fritz+Fränzi (the kindergarten issues), Familienspick, Newly Swissed and the three Swiss blogs.
   - The only published rate found is lafamily.ch: CHF 500 per sponsored article per site. A test buy could be one article on vaudfamille + genevefamille on "livre personnalisé pour accompagner un changement (déménagement, petit frère)".
   - Every paid piece must be labelled and carry nofollow links. That is also what the blogs themselves require.
6. **Monthly:** re-run the LLM queries, record the cited domains, and drop channels that never show up in citations.

Open owner decisions:
- (a) Are paid AI-directory listings OK?
- (b) What is the budget for lafamily and blog posts?
- (c) Does MagicalStory meet Startupticker's eligibility criteria?
