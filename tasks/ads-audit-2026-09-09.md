   ABOVE on 6/7, expected CTR ABOVE on 4/7. Nothing on the ads side fixes this; it is the S3 stream (hero keyword match,
   trust signals, mobile, render-blocking trim). QS lags ~2 weeks behind landing-page changes.
2. **`kinderbuch selbst gestalten` is the only keyword with BELOW ad relevance** (QS 5) — and it is the biggest keyword by
   far: 758 impr / 56 clicks / CHF 50.63 and **2 «Trial story completed»** (verified per keyword × conversion action;
